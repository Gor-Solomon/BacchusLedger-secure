- W01 put 10,100 L in the tank and bottled 9,630 L: 450 L lost and 20 L left, and nobody could say which step lost too much. [Dataset A6.2]
- Qvevri rule: by law "qvevri wine" must be fermented and matured in the qvevri up to 31 December of the year of pressing. [Challenge Book]
- The Kakheti PDO zone municipalities are Akhmeta, Telavi, Gurjaani, Sighnaghi, Dedoplistskaro, Kvareli, Lagodekhi and Sagarejo. [Kakheti PDO spec]
- The Kakheti PDO specification (§12) requires 8 journals: vineyard accounting, grape receiving, grape processing, vintage, storage and movement, bottling, laboratory analysis, tasting commission. [Kakheti PDO spec]
- Climate at reference point M1 Kvareli, Dec 2024–Nov 2025: rainfall 603.8 mm, summer mean 24.3 °C (regional model data, ERA5). [Dataset A1]
- Soil 0–30 cm at M1: pH 6.7 (SoilGrids). [Dataset B2]
- Generalised qvevri shape: height 180 cm, neck Ø60 cm, max Ø137.46 cm, about 1,500 L. [Dataset A5]
- Normal loss per stage used by the app: first racking 3.0%, racking 1.5%, filtration 0.6%, bottling 0.3%, stock check 0.3%, transfer 1.0% (editable in Settings). [DEMO ASSUMPTION]
- The dataset has no farmers: Demo Farmer 1–5 and the split of W01 batch 1 (16,000 kg) into 5,200 / 4,300 / 3,800 / 2,700 kg are invented for the demo. [DEMO ASSUMPTION]
- Tank sizes: F-1, F-2 Ø230 × 290 cm; T1-1…T1-5 Ø220 × 290 cm; BT-1 Ø210 × 290 cm; T3-1…T3-3 Ø110 × 160 cm. [DEMO ASSUMPTION]
- ABV: W01 batch 1 13.5%, W03 batch 3 12.8%. [DEMO ASSUMPTION]
- LIVE range alerts: fermentation temperature 20–30 °C for red and qvevri amber wine, 12–20 °C for white wine; storage temperature 10–18 °C; density 0.990–1.130 SG; an alert fires after 3 readings in a row outside the range. [DEMO ASSUMPTION]
- LIVE rule thresholds: level drop above 20 L within 1 h with no operation; stuck if density changes less than 0.001 in 48 h while SG is above 1.010. [DEMO ASSUMPTION]
- LIVE mode data comes from a simulator, is stored only in live_events and is never used by journals, losses or ROI numbers. Real sensors are tagged SENSOR. [SIMULATED]
- Potential alcohol ≈ Initial Brix × 0.59 (approximation). [Calculated]
- Fermentation progress = hydrometer ABV ÷ potential ABV × 100%. [Calculated]
